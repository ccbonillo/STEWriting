# English Writing Rules (STE-Compliant)

This document details the implementation of ASD-STE100 rules for English.

---

## 1. Sentence Limits
- **Procedures:** Maximum 20 words per sentence.
- **Descriptive text:** Maximum 25 words per sentence.
- **Paragraphs:** Maximum 6 sentences per paragraph. Exactly one topic per paragraph.

---

## 2. Verbs & Voice
- Use the **active voice** (Rule 3.6).
- Use **imperative mood** for procedure steps: "Open the valve."
- Permitted tenses:
  - Infinitive
  - Imperative
  - Simple Present ("The motor runs.")
  - Simple Past ("The light came on.")
  - Simple Future ("The unit will stop.")
  - Past participle **only** as an adjective ("The damaged seal.")
- **Avoid:**
  - Progressive forms ("is running", "was checking").
  - Perfect forms ("has installed", "had completed").
  - Phrasal verbs ("turn off" -> "deactivate" or "stop"; "set up" -> "install" or "configure").

---

## 3. Noun Clusters
- Multi-word nouns must not exceed **3 words** (Rule 2.1).
- Example:
  - Not STE: "system storage capacity alert threshold" (5 words)
  - STE: "threshold for system storage alerts"

---

## 4. Clarity & Punctuation
- Do not use semicolons (Rule 8.1).
- Spell out Latin abbreviations (Rule GR-6):
  - e.g. -> for example
  - i.e. -> that is
  - etc. -> and others / or similar items
- Always place conditions before instructions (Rule 5.4):
  - "If the alarm sounds, press the reset button."
